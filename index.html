Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

What happens to the names you type

The calculation runs entirely in your browser. Names are not sent to a server, saved, or logged by this site. Refreshing or closing the page clears them.

No accounts or cookies

The site has no sign-up, sets no cookies, and uses no advertising trackers.

Anonymous analytics

Vercel Web Analytics counts visits (page views, referrer, country, device and browser) without cookies or personal profiles. The names you type are never part of this data.
